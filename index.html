What does it sit on?

Pick a fabric, detail or colour to see which pieces it shows up on. Index compares that with the season's overall mix. 100 = the same as the season mix, 200 = twice as likely. For example, "silk satin → slip dress, index 320" means silk satin appears on slip dresses 3.2× more than you'd expect.
